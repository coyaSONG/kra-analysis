## Limits And Next Step

These are local source-clock and hash-lineage checks for October inputs. They
do not establish that October-captured historical versions existed before their
own historical races, independent raw-capture timestamps, complete career
history, or error-free future data. The HTML-only tier and unknown conditions
remain analytical limitations. No missing values or withheld observations were
filled from post-race data.

Together with PR108's entry/May-extension/feature/join checks, the fixed October
development link now has the planned source reconstruction evidence. Before
fitting, specify and review one fixed same-learner, same-cohort 24-versus-34
column recipe, including missingness and exception policy. Do not automatically
approve a recipe because source replay passed. The training review registry
remains unchanged and `training_approved=false`.

Once used for fitting or selection, these 50 races cannot independently validate
the resulting candidate. Any improvement claim requires a separately registered
future full population and timely published single-combination predictions.
Preserve the current full-block M0/API27 results; do not retune the same small
block repeatedly or replace its accuracy with diagnostic subgroup scores.
